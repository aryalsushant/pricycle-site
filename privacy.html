Privacy Policy

PriCycle · Last updated 18 September 2026

PriCycle does not collect any data. There is no account, no server and no analytics. Everything you log stays in an encrypted database on your phone, and the app makes no network requests at all.

What we collect

Nothing. PriCycle has no backend service and makes no network requests, so no information about you or your use of the app is transmitted, collected or received by the developer or by anyone else.

This includes the health information you enter — cycle dates, flow, symptoms, moods, sexual activity and notes — as well as any identifier, device information, location, contact details or usage statistics. None of it is gathered.

What the app stores, and where

What you log is written to a database on your device, encrypted with SQLCipher. The encryption key is generated on the device and kept in the operating system keychain, never inside the database and never anywhere else.

The database is explicitly excluded from iCloud and Android device backups, so your history does not travel off the phone inside a routine backup.

If you turn on the app lock, your PIN is never stored. What is stored is a scrypt hash that can check a PIN but cannot be turned back into one.

Analytics, tracking and advertising

There are none. The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or advertising SDK of any kind, and no third-party code that reports on your use of it.

Nobody, including the developer, can see what you log, when you open the app, or whether you use it at all.

Sharing with third parties

Your data is never shared, because it is never collected. There are no third parties with access to it: no processors, no service providers, no analytics vendors and no advertising partners.

Reminders, if you turn them on, are scheduled and delivered by your own phone. Nothing is registered with a push notification service, and the app works entirely offline.

Exports you create yourself

You can ask the app to produce a backup of what you have logged. That file is encrypted with a password you choose and handed to your device share sheet for you to send or save wherever you decide.

This only ever happens when you ask for it. Once you hand that file to another app or service, where it goes and how it is protected is governed by that service, not by PriCycle. Keep the password safe: without it the backup cannot be opened, and it cannot be recovered.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept on your device for as long as you choose to keep it. You can clear individual days at any time from within the app.

Deleting PriCycle removes its database along with it, permanently. Because there is no copy on any server, there is nothing to request the deletion of and nobody to ask — uninstalling the app is a complete deletion.

For the same reason there is no consent to withdraw, no data to export on request, and no access request to make: you already hold everything there is.

Children

PriCycle is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Not medical advice

PriCycle is a record-keeping tool. Its predictions are arithmetic estimates worked out on your device from the cycles you have logged, and are labelled as estimates wherever they appear.

They are not medical advice, not a diagnosis and not a method of contraception. Talk to a doctor or another qualified clinician about your health, and before making any medical decision.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will ship with an app update and the date above will change. Because the app collects nothing, any change here would follow a change in what the app does, not a change in what is done with data already held.
